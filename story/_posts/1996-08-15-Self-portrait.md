小小艺术家就有模仿大师的新潮想法，给自己也做一个自画像。自己就是模特，对吧。

A little artist with fresh, master-inspired ideas — why not create a self-portrait? After all, the best model is oneself, right?

下一半幅是以弟弟为模特，画了他的睡姿。

The below half features my younger brother as the model — I captured his sleeping posture in the sketch.
