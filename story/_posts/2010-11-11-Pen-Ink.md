小时候喜欢用铅笔照着小人书画里面的人物，什么常遇春，徐达。长大后有了美工笔（那种弯尖的钢笔），我开始喜欢用它写字，因为它可以画出不一样的粗细线条。漫画里的一些细节用它比较容易实现。多年以后，我已经买不到小时候的那种质量很好的美工笔了。有就比没有强，来试试吧。

As a child, I loved to trace the characters in my comic books with a pencil, like Chang Yuchun and Xu Da. Later, when I got art pens (those curved-nib fountain pens), I started to enjoy writing with them because they allowed me to draw lines of varying thicknesses. They were also better at capturing the details in comics. Years later, I can no longer find those high-quality art pens from my childhood. But something is better than nothing, so give it a try.
