想让闺女熟悉英语，给她看了一些迪士尼的动画片。其实某些是我学英语的时候下载的电影素材。前一阵子去广州，正好发现也这样的双语图书在卖，于是回到北京就买了一套。狮子王的电影闺女和我都比较喜欢，翻开书我就有了画一下电影人物的想法。辛巴自然是必须要画的。

I wanted my daughter to become more familiar with English, so I showed her some Disney animations. In fact, some of these were movies I had downloaded as learning materials when I was studying English. A while ago, during a trip to Guangzhou, I came across bilingual books like these for sale. So, after returning to Beijing, I bought a set. My daughter and I both love `The Lion King`, and when I opened the book, I immediately had the idea of drawing characters from the movie. Naturally, Simba was a must to paint.
