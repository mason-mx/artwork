画笔、画板、画架都买了，继续画画的心不会停。给闺女一个熏陶，也给自己满足。人像不好画，特别画的像更不容易。这次以我的偶像为画的主角。

I bought pencils, papers, and an easel—my passion for painting won’t stop. It’s both an artistic influence for my daughter and a source of personal fulfillment for me. Portraits are challenging to sketch, especially capturing a true likeness. This time, I chose my idol as the subject of my painting.
