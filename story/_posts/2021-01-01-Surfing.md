夏天了，带着朋友公司的产品，我们三个人去Long Bay冲浪。我试着尽量用一笔完成最多的画中的主题。左边是老秦，右边是我，两个冲浪板都是老梁带来的。看，上面还有North的logo呢。

Summer's here, and the three of us went surfing at Long Bay, carrying products from my friend's company. I tried to capture as much of the scene as possible in one stroke. On the left is Lao Qin, on the right is me, and both surfboards were brought by Lao Liang. Look, there's even the North logo on them!
