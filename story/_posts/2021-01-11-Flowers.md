开始探索油画的世界。从图书馆借的书，摆上新买的颜料，支上野餐用的桌子，我试着学习调色，于是第一个自习的油画完成了，还得到了邻居的夸奖呢。

I started exploring the world of acrylic painting. Borrowing books from the library, setting up my newly bought paints on a picnic table, I tried learning color mixing. Eventually, I completed my first self-taught acrylic painting—and even received compliments from my neighbors!
