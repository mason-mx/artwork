<p>从画中能感觉到狗对主人的思念和不舍，于是我在书中选了这个作为临摹的对象。而发生在自己身上的事情，也把自己印象为一条遭弃的小狗——泪眼婆娑，忘不了主人。</p>

<p>From the painting, I could feel the dog's longing and reluctance to part with its owner, so I chose this as my subject for copying in the book. The events in my own life also made me see myself as an abandoned puppy—eyes filled with tears, unable to forget its owner.</p>
