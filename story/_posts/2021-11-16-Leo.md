<p>前一阵子，狮子座的过生日。最近手里有的是一个涂黑的画布，一直在想画怎么样的画去配合黑色的背景。于是尝试着从来没画过的抽象风格。多彩的色条，虽然无法理解为何那么着色，但呈现的效果却出奇的好：既有狮子的威武，又有能掩盖真实色调的单薄和蛮野。所以画出来的狮子又可爱，又庄严。</p>

<p>A while ago, it was Leo's birthday. Recently, I had a black-painted canvas in hand and kept wondering what kind of painting would complement the dark background. So, I decided to try an abstract style I had never attempted before. The colorful streaks, though seemingly random in their placement, created an unexpectedly stunning effect—capturing both the lion’s majesty and a wild, untamed quality that conceals its true colors. As a result, the lion I painted turned out both adorable and dignified.</p>
