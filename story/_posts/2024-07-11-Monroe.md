玛丽莲梦露一直就在我的清单里面，可惜一直没有鼓起勇气去画她。毕竟人像画比较难，而且真要画的不像，别人很容易看出来。但从草稿开始，脑子里面那个形象慢慢地在纸上清晰起来，感觉真的很好，也是我第一次用照片做素材画的素描。

Marilyn Monroe had always been on my list, but I never had the courage to draw her. Portraits are difficult, and if they don’t resemble the person, it’s immediately noticeable. However, as I started with the sketch, the image in my mind gradually took shape on paper. It felt amazing—this was also my first time using a photo as a reference for a pencil sketch.
