第二次来迪士尼是在美国洛杉矶。有一个游戏项目是学着画画，那必须得去。一开始不太知道画谁，所以底稿打的不好。后来都是为了更好看和接近人物，我不得不添加了很多多余的线条。看来不给橡皮是有道理的，是给我们一个理念：一开始构图和完成之间的每一步都至少在作者的脑海了规划好了。

My second visit to Disneyland was in Los Angeles, USA. There was a ride where you could learn to draw, so I had to go. At first, I didn't really know who to draw, so my initial sketch wasn't very good. Later, to make it look better and more like the character, I had to add a lot of unnecessary lines. It seems there's a reason they don't provide erasers; it gives us a concept: every step between the initial composition and the final product must be well-planned in the artist's mind.
