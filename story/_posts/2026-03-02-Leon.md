这个电影是我最欢的之一。第一次看它的时候是我上大学的时候，之后就一直不忍心再看一遍，因为故事是很好可惜结局有点悲惨。后面看让雷诺的电影都是偏喜剧的类型，比如《你丫闭嘴》和《时空穿梭》。
近几年，电影的片尾曲《Shape of my heart》进了喜欢的播放清单，似乎再提醒我该再和《这个杀手不太冷》发生点什么——把人物放到画里面。这就不得不再看一遍电影，因为我想怎么能表现的好些：不画眼睛却还要人物性格鲜明。

This movie is one of my favorites. I first watched it when I was in college, and I've never been able to bear watching it again since, because the story is great but the ending is a bit tragic. Later, I watched mostly comedies of Jean Reno's films, like *Shut Up!* and *Time Travel*.

In recent years, the film's end credits song, *Shape of My Heart*, has been on my playlist, seemingly reminding me to do something with *Léon: The Professional* again—to draw the characters on the paper. This forced me to watch the movie again, because I was trying to figure out how to portray it better: without drawing eyes, yet still making the characters distinct.
