我女朋友有很好的身材，特别是美背和浑圆的屁股。可惜我没法拿着照片或者看着真人把她画下来。所以我在网上搜索了好久，包括裸体的图片和可以临摹的人体素描。终于发现了一个素材，我可以稍加发挥一下。以后可以想她时候，看看这个我自己画的素描，里面包含了好看的全部，甚至头发和发质看起来都很像。

My girlfriend has a great figure, especially her beautiful back and round butt. Unfortunately, I can't draw her from a photo or by looking at her in person. So I searched online for a long time, including nude pictures and figure sketches to copy. Finally, I found some material that I could work with a little. Later, when I miss her, I can look at this sketch I drew myself; it captures all her beauty, even the hair and texture look very similar.
