不是“一眼看尽”的才是好的艺术作品。画画和书法都适用这个检验方法。

当你看见，你开始注意了。然后每当你走进一点，就能发现更多的值得看的东西。到了近前，然后再后退，一些东西还值得回味。这个走上前和离开的过程都有不一样的体会，视觉上，心理上。

Good art isn't necessarily something you can "see everything at a glance." This method applies to both painting and calligraphy.

When you first see something, you begin to pay attention. Then, with each step you take closer, you discover more to see. Reaching a closer look and then stepping back reveals things worth savoring. This process of moving forward and then moving away offers different experiences, both visually and psychologically.
