怎么能想到，不是3个月，3年后，而是时隔30年（人生有几个30年），我会再次来个和同学们一起画画的课堂。只是对面没有了石膏体和各种静物了，而是活生生的模特。

回头想一想，只有上大学和刚毕业那会，基本就是停笔了。大学只有一次，那是给我们计算机系自己迎新晚会做的艺术字横幅。后面再捡起来这个爱好是女儿出生后。直到出国的这些年，有了更多的业余时间，这个兴趣才得以更好地发展和进步。

Who could have imagined that, not in three months, not three years later, but 30 years later (how many 30-year periods does one have in a lifetime), I would be back in a drawing class with my classmates. Only this time, instead of plaster casts and still life objects, there were living models.

Looking back, I basically stopped drawing only during university and right after graduation. In university, I only did it once, for an artistic banner I made for our computer science department's welcome party. I picked up this hobby again after my daughter was born. It wasn't until these years abroad, with more free time, that this interest was able to develop and progress better.
